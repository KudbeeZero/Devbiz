How to play

9 fixed paylines are always active — bet a per-line amount and hit SPIN. Match symbols left-to-right on a line (Wild substitutes for anything but Scatter) to win.

Paytable (× your per-line bet)

Symbol3×4×5×

Land 3+ Scatters anywhere on the grid: instant scatter pay (2×/5×/25× total bet) and the Bonus Wheel spins. The wheel's cash wedges pay a multiple of your total bet; its four jackpot wedges (Mini/Minor/Major/Grand) pay whatever that meter has ticked up to, then reset it. Every spin quietly feeds all four meters — that's why they keep climbing.

This build plays with demo chips only. The jackpot meters are a local, single-browser demo — nothing here is a shared/live progressive across players (that needs a real backend), and no real money or wallet funds are ever involved. "Connect Wallet" is a read-only Phantom preview only.
