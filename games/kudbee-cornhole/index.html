Controls: press and drag over the board to place the aiming ring, then release to throw. The ring sways, so time your release. Or use the arrow keys to aim and Space to throw. S switches between slide and flop throws. P pauses, M toggles sound, R restarts. A bag in the hole scores 3, a bag on the board scores 1, and the lower score cancels the higher. First to 21 wins.
