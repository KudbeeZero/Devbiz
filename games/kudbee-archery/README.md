# Kudbee Archery

Read the wind, hold your breath, hit the gold. Zero-build Canvas 2D, no dependencies.

- **Levels (the Tour):** 48 levels in eight worlds of six, laid out like the Kudbee Birds worlds - each world ends in a crowned **BOSS** level. *Village Green* (morning, the photo range), *Forest Glade* (late sun through painted pines), *Mountain Pass* (sunset, snow-capped peaks), *Lantern Night* (a floodlit range under the stars: moon, a far town's lights, fireflies), **THINK BOX** and three crypto worlds, **BITCOIN CITY**, **SOLANA BEACH** and **ALGORAND PEAKS** (below). PLAY on LEVELS opens the **level map**: one world per page, painted as a cartoon world in the Kudbee Birds style - a big sky with a sun (or a cratered moon) and slow rays, puffy outlined clouds drifting past, three layers of rolling hills, and the world's own props standing in the gaps between the tiles (straw targets with arrows in them, lollipop trees and a fence, cartoon pines, snow-capped peaks, a far town and a string of glowing paper lanterns with fireflies, crate forts). The still layers are painted once into an offscreen canvas per world and screen size. Six big tiles (number, name, stars, or a padlock; the boss tile is gold with a crown), < 1/8 > to page or swipe sideways, tap a tile to play it, BACK (or Esc) to the menu. Each level has its own twist: calm or strong wind, gusts that change arrow to arrow, a smaller face (40 cm at 30 m up to 80 cm at 70 m), a boss that slides along a rail, one that swings on ropes, or one that orbits a hub; up to 70 m and 5 ends for the bosses. The boss wears a gold crown on its butt. Every level has 1/2/3-star targets tuned with bots of three skill levels (`eval/calibrate.mjs`). One star opens the next level; clearing a boss opens the next world. The results card reads LEVEL CLEARED! / WORLD n COMPLETE! / TOUR COMPLETE! with NEXT LEVEL, RETRY and LEVELS. The menu shows a medal per world (stars, and a crown once its boss falls) and what's next. Saves from the earlier 15-level tour move onto the new layout with nothing lost, and 24-, 30- and 36-level saves keep every star. The menu shows the eight world medals in two rows of four.
- **THINK BOX (world 5):** a puzzle world that opens as soon as you beat the world 1 boss (the Village Cup), so it can be played alongside worlds 2-4. Wooden crates stand in front of the boss and stop an arrow dead (BLOCKED!, 0 points, the arrow stays in the crate): *Peephole* (the gold through a window in a crate wall), *Star Box* (a gold STAR BOX on the wall smashes for a sure 10, once an end), *Shutter* (a crate cart rolls to and fro across the face - loose when the gold is clear), *Two Windows* (the boss rides a rail behind a wall - hit it as it passes a window), *Criss-Cross* (two carts cross in front of the face) and the boss, *The Big Box* (a swinging boss behind a window at 50 m, with a STAR BOX up top). The crates are real 3D blocks (window jambs show their depth) drawn as tiles with frames, planks, braces and nails; the wind pennants stand in front of a wall so you can still read them. The calibration bots play these the way a player would - they take the STAR BOX and wait until the shot they mean is clear of the crates - and the star targets come from them.
- **The crypto worlds (6-8):** three neon worlds in a row. Each has its own coin turning on its COIN BLOCK (the crypto STAR BOX: +10 once an end, "COIN BLOCK! cashed in"), its own glowing chain blocks for crates, and its coins drifting over the range. **Themed only:** everything they pay is the game's own gems, with no real coins, wallets, tokens or money. (Anything real, such as payments, tokens or blockchain, is an owner-only `OWNER-OK` gate.)
  - **BITCOIN CITY (world 6)** opens once you beat the world 2 boss (the Forest Crown). It is an orange-neon night skyline with lit towers, a billboard whose candle chart only goes up, a rocket on its way to the moon, orange B coins and a glowing grid on the grass. Levels: *Genesis Block* (the gold through a window in the chain, a COIN BLOCK up top), *HODL* (a small face at 50 m), *Pump & Dump* (a swinging boss), *Gas Fees* (gusts arrow to arrow), *Bull Run* (a fast rail) and the boss, *The Whale* (on a rail behind a chain wall with two windows, a COIN BLOCK above).
  - **SOLANA BEACH (world 7)** opens off The Whale. It is a synthwave sunset: a striped retro sun sinking into a violet sea, palms, and a purple-to-mint grid on the sand. Everything is quick here, and it brings a new mover, the **ORBIT** (the boss goes round a hub on a flat loop, rising and falling as it goes, shown by a glowing ring and an arm). Levels: *Fast Lane* (the fastest rail), *Proof of History* (a shutter that ticks across the face), *High Throughput* (a new gust every arrow at 50 m), *Orbit*, *Parallel* (two windows, a quick boss and a COIN BLOCK) and the boss, *Sunset Validator* (an orbit at 50 m in a gusty sea breeze).
  - **ALGORAND PEAKS (world 8)** opens off the Sunset Validator. It is a polar night with the aurora rippling in green, teal and violet, faceted crystal peaks, a snow field, falling flakes and black-and-white A coins. Long range, small faces and no luck, just precision. Levels: *Pure Stake* (a 73 cm face at 50 m), *Finality* (a tiny window in an ice wall at 50 m, a COIN BLOCK), *Aurora Rail* (a rail at 70 m), *Frost Gale* (a gale that changes every arrow), *Crystal Orbit* (a smaller boss on a quick orbit) and the last boss, *The Peak* (five ends at 70 m, orbiting).
- **THE CUP (competition):** a knockout tournament of 8 archers: quarter-final, semi-final and final, each a duel to 6 set points. You face 7 named CPU rivals from a pool of 15, from ROOKIE RAY up to LADY LONGBOW. Each rival has their own rating, which sets how much their sight wobbles and how well they read the wind. You meet the weakest first, and the top seed is drawn on the far side. The other ties of each round are shot out by bots, so the favourites usually (not always) come through to meet you.
  - **The cups:** **BRONZE** (18 / 30 / 30 m) is open from the start, **SILVER** (30 / 50 / 50 m) opens when you win BRONZE, and **GOLD** (50 / 70 / 70 m) opens when you win SILVER. Winning a cup pays 40 / 80 / 150 gems the first time and 10 after that.
  - **On screen:** the menu shows the three cups and the one in progress. The HUD names your rival. After each tie the results card shows the whole bracket filling in, with NEXT ROUND (or NEW CUP once you're out or have won). A cup in progress is saved, and a hand-edited bracket is dropped. `KAR.Cup` in `sim.js`.
- **Tutorial:** the first two levels coach a new player, one card at a time (each shown once): aim at the gold (with a marker on it); hold your breath; then a gust and an AIM HERE marker at the exact hold-off with an arrow showing the wind carrying the shot to the gold; then "now read it yourself". **AIM HELP:** fail a still level twice without ever clearing it and the results card says AIM HELP IS ON; on the next tries the first arrow of each end shows an AIM HELP marker at the hold-off for that wind and your own bow and arrows (it follows gusts). Clearing the level puts it away (the count goes back to 0). Moving targets and crate walls never get it - those are read yourself.
- **Trophies:** 29 lifetime goals on a TROPHIES screen (menu, next to GEAR; nine to a page with progress bars): arrows shot, tens, X's, gold streaks, ROBIN HOODs, world bosses and tour stars, 3 stars at 70 m, duel wins and beating the LEGEND CPU, balloons popped and a 2,000 BALLOON POP, 90 on the SWINGER, 7 dailies, smashing a STAR BOX, all three crypto bosses (TO THE MOON), all eight bosses, all 144 tour stars, winning a cup and the GOLD CUP. Progress is read straight off the saved stats, so players keep credit for what they've already done; a trophy is announced the moment it's reached with a gold card at the top (and a chime and buzz), once. Badges only - no stat changes. `src/ach.js`.
- **Daily quests + gems:** three quests a day (the same three for everyone, picked from the UTC day, one per kind - e.g. shoot 20 arrows, hit 5 tens, 3 golds in a row, pop 25 balloons, a x3 combo, clear a level with 2+ stars, win a duel, play the DAILY, 60+ on the SWINGER, 100+ in a RANGE), counted from your own arrows in any mode. Finishing one drops in a card; CLAIM on the QUESTS screen (menu, top right; a red dot when something's ready) pays 10-20 gems, and claiming all three pays a 30-gem bonus. Gems are in-game only.
- **The agent (ROBIN):** a coach that rides along. It reads your last arrows (where each landed against the gold, the wind it flew in, how far you drew, how long you held) and names the fault in plain words: the wind is winning (hold off more), too much hold-off, short draws dropping low, grouping high/low, holding full draw too long, a wobbly hold (use your breath). A tip card slides in at the bottom left after an arrow when it sees something (at most the same tip every 4 arrows; switch it off on the AGENT screen). The AGENT screen (menu button, pause, the `/` key, or a `#agent` link) plots your last 9 arrows on a target and lists what's next: the quest closest to done, the next level short of 3 stars, the trophy closest to done, and kit that answers your fault. **ASK ROBIN:** four questions (how do I read the wind? which bow & arrows? how do I get 3 stars? how am I doing?) answered from your own game: your wind error against the real drift at each distance, your kit and gems, the next level's 3-star score as points per arrow, your lifetime tens/X/streak. After every round (not BALLOON POP) the results card carries ROBIN's debrief (average, golds, the thing to work on); tap it for the AGENT screen. ROBIN remembers your last 12 arrows in the save (a forged entry is dropped) and chirps when it has a tip. Rules, not a model: it runs offline and sends nothing anywhere. `src/agent.js`.
- **Small things that add up:** a **level card** drops in as a tour level starts (the level, its twist, distance and ends, and the score for each star beside your best; it goes when you draw). Every star you earn for the first time pays **5 gems** (shown on the results card). A **day streak** counts the days in a row you shoot an arrow (UTC): day 3 pays 15 gems and every 7th day 40, with a card; the QUESTS screen shows it. The results card **plots your group** on a little face. A new end calls its wind at the bottom ("THE WIND HAS SWITCHED SIDES!" when it flips) and the gauge flashes, and END COMPLETE says how far **ahead of or behind your best** you are. PAUSE has **LEVELS** on a tour level and, on a touch screen, **AIM SPEED** (SLOW / NORMAL / FAST: how far the sight follows your finger). On the results card **N** = next level, **R** = again, **L** / **Esc** = the level map. With *reduce motion* set on the device the screen doesn't shake, the confetti is a sprinkle and the chips stop pulsing.
- **Bows & arrows:** gems buy bows and arrows that *handle* differently - none is simply better. Bows: CLUB RECURVE (free), HUNTER LONGBOW (30: a steadier, forgiving sight but a slower arrow that drifts more), OLYMPIC RECURVE (60: fast and flat, twitchy sight), COMPOUND (100: fast and calm, but a held full draw tires fast). Arrows: ALUMINIUM (free), CARBON (25: cuts the wind, but a short draw drops hard), HEAVY CEDAR (40: shrugs off wind and short draws, but slow - lead movers more), X10 PRO (80: fast and barely drifts, needs a full draw). Every bow is sighted in for its own speed, so a still-air shot always lands where you aim; the kit changes wind drift, sight wobble, stamina at full draw and what a short draw costs. Tap new kit to see it, then BUY (no accidental spends); tap kit you own to use it. Open from QUESTS or the GEAR locker. The CPU and the DAILY (same for everyone) always shoot club kit. `src/quest.js`, `KAR.KIT` in `sim.js`.
- **Gear locker:** bow finishes (Club Oak, Black Carbon, Royal Blue, Cherry, Gold Leaf), fletching (Team, Flame, Lime, Rainbow, Gold) and sight colours (Classic, Ice, Ruby, Gold) open as your star total (levels + ranges) grows - stars are never spent, and gear is looks only. The results card announces new kit; the CPU keeps club kit.
- **Phones:** the game fills a landscape phone edge to edge - the 960 x 640 stage keeps its layout and a wider screen shows more scenery either side (no black bars), with the score, wind and buttons riding out to the real corners. Touch aims like a trackpad: put your finger down anywhere, the sight starts on the gold and follows your drag at 0.7x, so your thumb never covers the target. While one finger draws, a second finger anywhere holds your breath (or tap the big BREATH button). Small print is ~25% bigger on touch screens. The menu uses a compact title and two rows of big buttons; the level map's tiles are big and you can swipe between worlds. Android: PLAY goes full screen and locks landscape (a corner button and the pause menu toggle it), and you get a short buzz at full draw, on the release and on golds (VIBRATE on/off in pause). iPhone Safari has no full-screen mode for pages, so the menu suggests Add to Home Screen - the game ships a web-app manifest and icons and opens full screen from there. The screen stays awake during a range, held upright you get a full-screen 'turn sideways' card (a range pauses under it), the Studio link only shows on the menu, and the render resolution matches the screen and steps down if frames run slow.
- **Modes:** *Range* — 5 ends of 3 arrows (max 150) at 18 / 30 / 50 / 70 m, 3 stars per range. *Duel* — you vs the CPU, alternate arrows, each end is worth 2 set points (1 each if level), first to 6 wins; CPU is ROOKIE / PRO / LEGEND. *Balloon Pop* — arcade: balloons drift across the range 25 m out, pushed by the wind and bobbing; lead the movers. 12 arrows; 100 a balloon (gold 250, and it refills 2 arrows); pops on consecutive arrows build a combo up to x5; an arrow flies on after a pop, so two lined-up balloons both go (and score double each). Best score is saved. *Swinger* — the 30 m boss hangs on two parallel ropes from a timber frame and swings like a pendulum (period 2π√(L/g) ≈ 3.1 s, staying upright and rising at the ends of the arc); 4 ends of 3 arrows, the swing grows each end, arrows ride in the boss and a miss off it flies through. Lead it. *Daily* — one Balloon Pop a day, seeded from the UTC date (#1 = 2026-10-17), so the wind and the balloons are the same for everyone; today's best, first try and plays are saved, and SHARE makes a brag card with the score.
- **70 m unlock:** 70 m opens after 6 stars across 18/30/50 m (players who already have a 70 m score keep it); the results card announces it.
- **Bow draw:** a first-person recurve built in 3D around the sight, so it sways with your aim. The bow comes up as you start, the draw is quick off the brace and heavier into the anchor, the limbs bend back and the recurve tips unroll, the string folds into a V and the arrow slides back through the rest until its point clicks past the clicker. Hold too long and the draw shakes and creeps. The riser sits right of the sight so the gold stays in view. Both arms are shaded 3D limbs (creased sleeve, rolled cuff, a stitched leather arm guard with two buckled straps). The hands are illustrated once, each as ONE smooth silhouette (a Catmull-Rom outline through hand-placed points) with the fingers parted by fine inner lines, rounded shading along each finger, knuckle highlights, joint creases and nails, and placed by perspective, so there are no seams or squared-off finger ends. The nocked arrow lies on a slant: the anchor sits low and right of the eye, so the drawn shaft runs from the bottom-right corner up to the rest - solid, outlined and easy to see - instead of pointing straight down the line of sight. The bow hand is a fist on the grip (thumb up the riser toward the target, knuckles down the grip, four fingers curled round its edge, a braided finger sling) that springs open on the release; the draw hand hooks the index above the nock and middle + ring below round the string in front of a leather tab, little finger curled in and the thumb tucked under - its forearm is part of the same silhouette, so the wrist has no seam, and runs back into a rolled team-colour sleeve. Near the eye the arrow and string go soft and out of focus instead of turning into planks. On the release the arm follows through, the hand opens and the bow tips forward into the sling.
- **Release:** letting go kicks the bow forward and away; the limbs whip past brace and ring down, the string snaps and shivers (a blur either side of it), a streak and a puff of light leave through the sight, and the view jolts.
- **Controls:** press and hold (or hold **Space**) to draw, move the pointer (or arrow keys) to aim, release to loose. Hold **B** / **Shift** / right-click to steady your breath (about 2.4 s, then you gasp and the sight shakes). Hold the full draw too long and your arm tires. **P** pause · **M** mute · **R** restart. On touch, drag anywhere to aim (the sight follows your finger without sitting under it) and a second finger or the BREATH button holds your breath; the phone's long-press gestures are blocked on the play area, and if the browser cancels the touch mid-draw the draw is kept and the arrow still goes when the finger lifts.
- **Wind:** the gauge shows which way the wind pushes the arrow. The sight is zeroed for still air, so *aim into the wind*. It re-rolls every end and gusts per arrow; longer ranges get stronger wind.
- **Scoring:** ten rings (1–10), the centre dot is an **X** (a 10 that breaks ties). The face is 40 cm at 18 m, 80 cm at 30 m and 122 cm at 50/70 m.
- **Physics:** metres. Gravity plus quadratic drag against the *air* (so wind matters); a bisection solver zeroes the sight for each distance. Less than a full draw means a slower, lower arrow. Fixed seeds replay identically.
- **Camera:** one pinhole camera blends between a zoomed scope view (aiming) and a wide view that follows the arc, then punches back in on the hit.
- **Arrow cam:** golds (plus the odd 7 or 8, so the cam alone doesn't give it away), the last arrow of a range and the CPU's X ride the arrow in, in slow motion, with speed lines, then hold close on the hit. Press (or Space) to skip.
- **The landing:** arrows are real 3D objects (shaft, three spinning vanes, nock). On impact the point drives in, the shaft flexes from its flight line to a tail-up rest and quivers; it punches a hole and casts a shadow on the face. After an arrow-cam shot the camera swings round to a three-quarter view of the arrow standing out of the boss.
- **Moments:** a fresh hit kicks up chaff; consecutive 9s and better build a **GOLD STREAK** (the chime climbs a tone each time; best streak is saved); an arrow that lands in the hole of one already in the face this end is a **ROBIN HOOD** and splits it.
- **Last-arrow pressure:** on a range's final arrow the screen shows what you need for a new best and for 3 stars; in a duel, what the CPU needs on its last arrow to take the end.
- **Sound:** the core loop is **recorded** (CC0, credits in `assets/SFX_CREDITS.md`): one 190 KB sprite, `assets/sfx.mp3`, built by `tools/sfx-build.mjs` and mapped in `src/sfx.js`, loaded once and kept for offline play. The bow's creak and release, the arrow's swish, the thwack into the straw, a crowd's "ooh" and applause, each a few takes played at a slightly random pitch so no two shots sound alike. **The score is heard:** each ring lands a little tighter and brighter than the one below (5-8 add a woodblock tick climbing a step each), a 9 draws the crowd's "ooh", a 10 rings a bell, an X has its own call (a low boom, a two-note bell, a sparkle run, the crowd going up), and a gold streak lifts the chimes a tone each. Layered under the recordings, and standing in if the sprite can't load, the synthesized set: a bow creak that tightens with the draw (and settles to a faint strain at anchor), the clicker at full draw, the string slap + twang + limb thump on release, an arrow whoosh that falls in pitch as it flies, a straw thud with the shaft quivering, crowd (a groan when your last arrow misses), the results stars landing one by one on rising chimes, a calm loop with **a tune for each world** (its own key, tempo and lead: a backyard strum on the Village Green, slow and birdless at Lantern Night, a night synth in Bitcoin City; the map plays the world on its page), and an outdoor bed: wind that rises and brightens with the wind gauge, plus birds. A compressor keeps it loud on phone speakers. iOS: audio unlocks on touchend/click with a silent buffer inside the gesture, and the page asks for a "playback" audio session so the ringer switch doesn't silence it (Safari 16.4+). M or the speaker button mutes.
- **Feel:** a gold (10 or X) lands with a **hit-stop** - the world holds for 60-90 ms under a white flash (none with reduced motion) - on top of the arrow cam's slow motion. On Android the phone ticks as the limbs load (a third, two thirds) and gives a firmer tick at full draw.
- **CLOUD SAVE + THE LEAGUE (guest mode):** progress follows you. After your first finished round the game makes a **backup code** on the phone (`KDB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`, 80 random bits); the CLOUD SAVE button (menu, bottom left; a green dot once synced) shows it with COPY CODE, your league name (ARCHER + 4 characters from the code's hash; tap to rename), USE A CODE FROM ANOTHER PHONE, how the last sync went, and THE LEAGUE (top 8 by stars, you in green, or your rank). Every finished round syncs: read the cloud save, **merge** it with the phone's (the best of both: stars, bests, stats, trophies, gems, bows and arrows owned, cups, the newer daily and streak; the phone keeps its own settings), write it back on the revision it read - if another phone wrote in between it is refused with the newer save, merged again and written again. Typing a code on a new phone merges that player's progress in, nothing is lost on either side. A merged save goes back through the save's own checks. The server is the Kudbee leaderboard Worker (`leaderboard/`, `/api/save` + `/api/scores`); it stores a hash of the code, never the code. No Worker, no signal or a blocked request: nothing changes in play, the save stays on the phone and CLOUD SAVE says so. `src/cloud.js` (`window.KAR_API` overrides the server: `''` = this site, `null` = off). Going live: `leaderboard/README.md` → *Go live: guest mode*.
- **The app (offline + install):** `sw.js` keeps the whole game (page, code, font, pictures) so it opens and plays with no signal, from the home screen or the browser. It is network-first for the page and code (a deploy shows on the next load) and cache-first for pictures; bump its `V` to drop old copies. The manifest has an id, a maskable icon (the art inside Android's safe circle) and two phone screenshots for Android's install sheet, all rendered from the game by `tools/app-assets.mjs`. From the second visit, phones get an **INSTALL** button on the menu: Android fires the browser's own install prompt; iPhone Safari (which has none) gets a card showing Share -> Add to Home Screen -> open it. Once installed, used or waved off (GOT IT), the button stays gone.
- **Sharing:** every win can be shown off. Clearing a level or a world boss, finishing the tour, winning a cup round or the cup, a Daily score, and any won trophy (tap it on the TROPHIES screen) makes a **brag card**: a 1200x630 picture painted from the game's own art (the world's cartoon backdrop, the results panel, your score and stars, the cup / crown / coin / medal) by `src/share.js`. SHARE opens a SHARE screen that previews it: phones get SHARE PICTURE (the share sheet with the picture attached, so it lands in Messages / Instagram / WhatsApp as an image), and everyone gets X, Facebook, WhatsApp, COPY LINK and SAVE IMAGE. The line links to a small landing page per moment (`s/w1/` … `s/w8/`, `s/cup-bronze|silver|gold/`, `s/daily/`, `s/champion/`, `s/trophy/`) whose `og:image` is that moment's card (`/assets/og/archery/<slug>.jpg`), so a pasted link unfurls with the right picture; the page sends people straight on to the game. `tools/share-cards.mjs` renders those cards and writes the pages (bump its `VER` when they change). The game page itself keeps its full share card - Open Graph + Twitter/X `summary_large_image`, JSON-LD `VideoGame` - with `/assets/og/archery.jpg` rendered from the game by `tools/og-card.mjs` (bump the `?v=` when you regenerate it, so the apps refetch).
- **Ghost challenges:** any level result with points on it has a **CHALLENGE** (VS) button: it shares a link and a "CAN YOU BEAT ME?" card. The link carries the whole round - the level, the match seed, your name and every arrow (ring, X, where it hit) - so there is no server: `?c=` on the game's address. A friend who opens it sees the invite (who, their score and stars, which level); ACCEPT plays that level on that seed, so **the same wind for every arrow** (one roll per end and a gust per arrow, whatever anyone shoots). As each of their arrows lands, yours lands beside it as a see-through ghost, and the HUD keeps your score after the same number of arrows. The results read CHALLENGE WON! / ALL SQUARE! / CHALLENGE LOST with REMATCH (the same ghost again) and CHALLENGE to send theirs back. A level the friend hasn't opened is **practice**: it plays, but nothing is saved to their map. A link is never trusted: `src/ghost.js` checks every field against the level it names, refuses anything off, and adds the score up from the rings. It works offline too (the service worker serves the game for `?c=` links).
- **Focus:** a hidden tab pauses; a plain window blur (iframes, toolbars) only drops held input.

## Layout
`src/util.js` · `sim.js` (CFG, Flight, Score, Match, Stage + TOUR, Swing, Trick, Bot — DOM-free) · `ghost.js` (challenge links, DOM-free) · `render.js` · `ui.js` · `game.js` · `audio.js` · `sfx.js` (generated: the sprite's map).
