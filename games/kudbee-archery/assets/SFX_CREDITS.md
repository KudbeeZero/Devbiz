# Kudbee Archery — recorded sound effects

`sfx.mp3` is built by `tools/sfx-build.mjs` from the recordings below. Every source is
**CC0 1.0 (public domain)**, so no credit is required; it's given anyway, with thanks.

| Clips | Source | Author | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `swish1-3` (the arrow leaving) | [Swishes Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/swishes-sound-pack) — `swish-7/8/9.wav` | artisticdude | CC0 |
| `hit1-4` (the thwack), `body1-3` (the boss) | [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) — `impactPunch_medium_*`, `impactSoft_heavy_*` | Kenney | CC0 |
| `creak1-2` (the bow drawing) | [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) — `creak1/3.ogg` | Kenney | CC0 |
| `ooh1` (the near miss; layered into a crowd in code) | [OoOoOoOoOoOoOo](https://opengameart.org/content/oooooooooooooo) | AuraVoice | CC0 |
| `clap1-2` (the crowd) | [Applause in a large hall or church](https://opengameart.org/content/applause-in-a-large-hall-or-church) | expl0it3r | CC0 |
