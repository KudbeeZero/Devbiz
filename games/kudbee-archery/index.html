Controls: press and hold the mouse button or Space to draw the bow, move the pointer or use the arrow keys to aim, release to shoot. Hold B or the right mouse button to hold your breath and steady the sight. The wind gauge shows which way the wind pushes the arrow, so aim into it. P pauses, M toggles sound, R restarts. Ten rings, worth one to ten points; the centre is an X.
