How to play

Pick a bet size, hit DROP, and watch the chip fall through the peg field. It's a real Galton board: each peg is a 50/50 bounce, so drops land in the middle far more often than they land at the edges — that's exactly why the edge slots pay the biggest multipliers and the middle slots pay less than your bet back most of the time.

This build plays with demo chips only — nothing here moves real money. The gold "Connect Wallet" button is a read-only preview: it shows your real Phantom address (and real SOL balance, just for show) but never signs a transaction or touches your funds.

Tap the 🔒 Fair badge any time to see exactly how the odds are generated, and to verify past rounds yourself.
