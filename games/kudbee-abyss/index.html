How to play

Photosensitivity warning: this game has flashing lights, sudden flashes and a shaking screen.
  1. Tap a reply to answer. Every choice changes the story.
  2. Watch the rings: heart, resolve, trust… and dread. Too much dread and she comes apart.
  3. The phone is real: its flashlight drains the battery, and the dark is worse without it.
  4. Play the flute when it asks. Tap the lanes to the beat.
  5. Swipe left on the home screen for your Echoes. There are 26 endings.
